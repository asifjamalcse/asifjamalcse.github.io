## Research Projects

- **DentSeg-EDA:** 3D tooth segmentation in CBCT using enhanced dual attention. Published in *Neurocomputing*.
- **DentSegPlus:** Centroid and skeleton-guided tooth segmentation with cross-axis context modeling. Manuscript under submission.
- **Sparse-view 3D CT synthesis:** Projection-guided latent diffusion and refinement. Manuscript under submission.
- **Breast cancer segmentation:** Led Team SegMed’s winning project at the K-Health Medical AI Hackathon.
