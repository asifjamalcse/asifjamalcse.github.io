## About Me
I am a PhD researcher in Information and Communication Engineering at **Chosun University, South Korea**, where I expect to complete my degree in **February 2027**. My doctoral research is supervised by [Prof. Bumshik Lee](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=maY0q9IAAAAJ&hl=en) and is conducted with the [ENVI Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/envilab-kentech).
My research focuses on deep learning for medical image analysis and computer vision. My doctoral work develops anatomy-guided and context-adaptive methods for tooth localization and instance segmentation in 3D dental CBCT, combining centroid and skeleton guidance, dual-attention encoders, and axis-wise state-space models. I also work on latent diffusion for sparse-view 3D CT synthesis and have contributed to research in aerial object detection, multi-object tracking, semantic segmentation, and multimodal human action recognition.
I developed **DentSeg-EDA**, published in *Neurocomputing*, and am developing **DentSegPlus**, which combines centroid and skeleton guidance with cross-axis context modeling. I received the Grand Prize at the K-Health Medical AI Hackathon for breast cancer segmentation.
I previously served as a Lecturer at **Air University, Islamabad**, where I taught undergraduate and graduate courses and supervised student research. I am seeking postdoctoral opportunities in medical image analysis, computer vision, and deep learning after my expected graduation in February 2027.

## Research Interests
- **Medical Image Analysis:** 3D medical image segmentation, dental CBCT analysis, tooth localization and instance segmentation, sparse-view CT synthesis, breast cancer segmentation, and anomaly detection.
- **Computer Vision:** Object detection, semantic and instance segmentation, multi-object tracking, human action recognition, and aerial image analysis.
- **Deep Learning:** Convolutional neural networks, attention mechanisms and transformers, state-space models such as Mamba, diffusion and generative models, and multimodal learning.
- **Healthcare AI:** Privacy-preserving e-health data and clinically oriented AI applications.
