## About Me
I am a PhD researcher in Information and Communication Engineering at **Chosun University, South Korea**, and expect to graduate in **February 2027**. My doctoral research is supervised by [Prof. Bumshik Lee](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=maY0q9IAAAAJ&hl=en) and conducted with the [ENVI Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/envilab-kentech).
My research focuses on deep learning for medical image analysis and computer vision. My doctoral work develops anatomy-guided and context-adaptive methods for tooth localization and instance segmentation in 3D dental CBCT, combining centroid and skeleton guidance, dual-attention encoders, and axis-wise state-space models. I also work on latent diffusion for sparse-view 3D CT synthesis and have contributed to research in aerial object detection, multi-object tracking, semantic segmentation, and multimodal human action recognition.
I developed **DentSeg-EDA**, published in *Neurocomputing*, and am developing **DentSegPlus**, which combines centroid and skeleton guidance with cross-axis context modeling. I received the Grand Prize at the K-Health Medical AI Hackathon for breast cancer segmentation. I previously served as a Lecturer at **Air University, Islamabad**, teaching undergraduate and graduate courses and supervising student research.
<p><strong>I am seeking research (postdoctoral or research professor) opportunities in medical image analysis, computer vision, and deep learning after my expected graduation in February 2027.</strong></p>
