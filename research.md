## Research Interests

My research focuses on medical image analysis and computer vision, especially deep learning methods for 3D dental cone-beam CT (CBCT).

- 3D tooth localization and instance segmentation
- Anatomy-guided and context-adaptive representation learning
- Attention mechanisms and state-space models
- Medical image synthesis and reconstruction

## Current Research

### DentSegPlus: Centroid and Skeleton-Guided Tooth Segmentation with Cross-Axis Context Modeling

I am developing DentSegPlus with Prof. Bumshik Lee. The framework combines region localization, centroid and skeleton guidance, and individual tooth segmentation, using enhanced dual attention and axis-wise Mamba blocks.

**Status:** Under submission.

### Projection-Guided Latent Diffusion and Refinement for Sparse-View 3D CT Synthesis

I am collaborating with Fawad and Prof. Bumshik Lee on this project, which explores latent diffusion for synthesizing 3D CT volumes from sparse-view projections.

**Status:** Under submission.

## Selected Research

### DentSeg-EDA

Published in *Neurocomputing* in 2025, DentSeg-EDA uses enhanced dual attention in a staged framework for tooth instance segmentation in CBCT images.

[View the paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2025.131437)

### Breast Cancer Segmentation

I led Team SegMed in the 2024 K-Health Medical AI Hackathon. Our team developed a breast cancer segmentation approach and won the competition, receiving a KRW 3,000,000 prize.

## Research Environment

I am a PhD researcher in Information and Communication Engineering at Chosun University, working with the ENVI Lab at KENTECH under the supervision of Prof. Bumshik Lee. My PhD research is titled **“Anatomy-Guided and Context-Adaptive Representation Learning for Robust 3D Tooth Instance Analysis in Cone-Beam CT.”**
