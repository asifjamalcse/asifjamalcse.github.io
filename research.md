## Research Overview
My research focuses on deep learning for medical image analysis and computer vision. My doctoral work develops anatomy-guided and context-adaptive methods for tooth localization and instance segmentation in 3D dental cone-beam CT (CBCT). I also work on generative modeling for medical imaging, including latent diffusion for sparse-view 3D CT synthesis.
## Research Interests
- **Medical Image Analysis:** 3D medical image segmentation, dental CBCT analysis, tooth localization and instance segmentation, sparse-view CT synthesis, breast cancer segmentation, and anomaly detection.
- **Computer Vision:** Object detection, semantic and instance segmentation, multi-object tracking, human action recognition, and aerial image analysis.
- **Deep Learning:** Convolutional neural networks, attention mechanisms and transformers, state-space models such as Mamba, diffusion and generative models, and multimodal learning.
- **Healthcare AI:** Privacy-preserving e-health data and clinically oriented AI applications.
## Current Research Projects
### DentSegPlus: Centroid and Skeleton-Guided Tooth Segmentation with Cross-Axis Context Modeling
I am developing DentSegPlus with Prof. Bumshik Lee. It combines centroid and skeleton guidance, enhanced dual-attention encoders, and axis-wise state-space modeling to capture context across 3D dental CBCT volumes.
**Manuscript status:** Under submission to *Computers in Biology and Medicine*.
### Projection-Guided Latent Diffusion and Refinement for Sparse-View 3D CT Synthesis
In collaboration with Fawad and Prof. Bumshik Lee, this project explores latent diffusion and refinement for synthesizing 3D CT volumes from sparse-view projections.
**Manuscript status:** Under submission.
### Next Research Direction: Dental Anomaly Detection
My next research direction is graph-based unsupervised anomaly detection in dental imaging, using relationships among dental structures to identify abnormalities such as missing or impacted teeth.
## Selected Research Contributions
### DentSeg-EDA
DentSeg-EDA is a staged framework for 3D tooth segmentation in CBCT images using enhanced dual attention. The work was published in *Neurocomputing* in 2025.
[View the paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2025.131437)
### Breast Cancer Segmentation
I led Team SegMed in developing a breast cancer segmentation approach for the 2024 K-Health Medical AI Hackathon.
### Computer Vision Research
My collaborative work also includes aerial object detection and semantic segmentation, multi-object tracking, and multimodal human action recognition.
